ClipPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

ClipPilot is a personal short-video scheduling tool. It stores account tokens and scheduled posts on the machine that runs the app.

  • We use Google / Meta / TikTok OAuth only to upload videos and read basic publish stats you request.
  • Credentials and media stay on your ClipPilot host unless you share the public link.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • You can disconnect accounts anytime in ClipPilot Settings.
